# Expert stabilization of the ambitious run (2026-10-29/30)

## Problem and mechanism

In the ambitious main run (LDM, classical Muown, no weight decay, constant lr 8.5e-4), the routed-expert
output grows until single experts dominate their layers.

The weight spectra of the main run at step 850 and of the reproducer at step 900
(`scripts/korbi/campaign/expert_spectra.py`) show the cause.
- **One expert per layer collapses:**
  - Its stable rank |W|_F² / σmax² falls from ~200–300 to single digits.
  - Its fc1 and fc2 co-align: alignment 0.95, against 0.75 for the others.
  - Examples: reproducer layer 73, expert 39 (gain 10.0 vs median 0.30); main run layer 65, expert 49 (gain 9.1).
- **The norms stay flat:** Frobenius and row norms do not move, so caps on norms don't see the collapse.
- **The router is healthy:** load entropy ~0.998, max_vio < 1.
- **The pre-MLP norm gains** rise only 10–14 %.
- **It is step-driven, not token-driven:** the reproducer, with 8x fewer tokens per step, blows up at the same steps.
  This fits Muown's late rotation per step of ~1/√t, which does not depend on the lr.

The reproducer is `config/experiments/korbi/campaign_3b/ambrepro_mn5.yaml`: the production ambitious
model on 16 nodes, gbs 512, mbs 2, exit at step 900.

## Recommendation: `pab`, the bounded post-activation norm

Settings: `moe_expert_post_act_norm: true`, `mlp_post_act_norm: true`, `post_act_norm_floor: 1.0`.
The SwiGLU activation is divided by max(rms, 1), fused with the router weight. No lr or warm-up change.

### Stability

Largest routed-expert output (`moe/routed_expert_output_rms_max`) in the reproducer:

| step | 300 | 500 | 600 | 700 | 800 | 900 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| base | 0.13 | 0.88 | 2.33 | 4.08 | 5.88 | 6.73 |
| pab | 0.064 | 0.060 | 0.062 | 0.064 | 0.070 | 0.076 |
| pab_r (identical twin) | 0.064 | 0.061 | 0.063 | 0.065 | 0.069 | 0.070 |
| peri-LN | 0.046 | 0.067 | 0.071 | 0.079 | 0.086 | 0.090 |

- Mean expert output: base grows 0.056 → 0.24; pab stays at ~0.035.
- Router: min load entropy 0.985 in all runs.
- LDM under pab (`pab_r`): stream rms 1.83 → 0.47, write ratio 0.15, read gain 1.03 → 1.27.

### Loss, gap vs the rematch `ldm` (same layout, batches and seed)

| arm | s3 | s2 |
|---|---|---|
| pab | −0.005 to −0.008 in every 200-step window from step 400 to 4000; final −0.005 | ±0.002 throughout; final +0.000 |

- **Reproducer:** pab and pab_r trail base by +0.009 and +0.006 at steps 800–900. The cost is shrinking, and base has
  not yet degraded at that point.
- **Reproducer noise:** pab and pab_r have the same seed and data, yet differ by 0.033 at steps 100–200 and by 0.003
  at steps 700–800. The step-to-step scatter of their difference is only 0.001–0.002, so this is a steady offset
  between two trajectories. Reproducer loss gaps below ~0.003 are not resolvable.

**Cost at 64 nodes:** about −0.1 % speed and +0.4 GB. At 288 experts, memory use is 0.992 with pab against 0.977 without.

## What else was tried

All runs are in `screen5stab_s{2,3}`, generated by `gen_screen.py arms5stab`. Gaps are vs `ldm`.

| fix | s3 | s2 | notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| full post-act norm (pan) | +0.008 | +0.001 | stable in the reproducer, +0.018 there |
| stable-rank floor 25 / 50 / 100 | −0.001 / +0.001 / −0.000 | 50: cancelled | rank-1 deflation to the floor; neutral |
| spectral cap κ = 3 (σmax ≤ 3·σ_init) | −0.003 at step 3670 | pending | led pab by 0.014 at steps 200–400, then fell 0.003 behind from step 1600; binds < 1 % of fc2 after step 1000 |
| spectral cap κ = 5 | +0.002 | – | almost never binds |
| orthogonal expert directions (polar) | +0.021 | – | Stiefel-like; drift 0.003 per step, 2 iterations |
| peri-LN (`post_sublayer_norm`) | +0.001 (−0.025 … +0.039 per window) | −0.000 (−0.014 … +0.035) | erratic; ~2x grad-norm spikes; median grad norm +30–50 % |
| half lr | −0.007 | −0.008 | disallowed: no lr changes |
| g decay / v decay / clamp 5 | worse | | earlier rounds |

**Grad-norm spikes:** every arm has isolated single-step spikes of 3–14x the local median, at 3–10 per 1000 logged
points; peri-LN has 13–16. They come without loss spikes. The runaway does not show up in them: in base it is a
steady rise of the expert output at a normal spike rate.

**nGPT-like late decay:** no variant reproduced it. The orthogonal (Stiefel-like) constraint costs about 0.02.

## Implementation (Megatron, oellm/v0.19-hybrid)

**Post-act norm** (`fused_bias_swiglu.py`):
- `normed_swiglu` / `post_act_rms_norm`, with `floor` and `smooth`.
- Call sites: `experts.py`, `mlp.py`, `shared_experts.py`, `moonep.py`.
- Metrics: `moe_act/rms`, `moe_act/capped`.

**Muown expert bounds** (`emerging_optimizers.py`, `TensorParallelMuownClassical`, routed experts only, i.e. `allreduce=False`):

| setting | what it does |
|---|---|
| `muown_expert_stable_rank_floor` | exact rank-1 deflation to stable rank r_min; g follows the new row norms |
| `muown_expert_spectral_cap` | W scaled by min(1, κ·σ_init/σmax); σ_init = rms·(√m+√n) per shape from the init weights, all-reduced at optimizer construction; σmax by a 10-step power iteration |
| `muown_expert_orthogonal` | W = diag(\|W_i\|)·Q/\|Q_i\| with Q the polar factor of the row-normalized W; convergent cubic Newton–Schulz in fp32 until \|XXᵀ−I\| < 1e-5 |

**On Megatron-FSDP row shards**, all three bounds act on the full matrices at their owners, via the row exchange. Only
the change is sent back, and it is exactly 0 for uncapped matrices.

**Metrics:** `arch/muown/expert_<m>x<n>/{floored,srank,capped,sigma_over_cap,drift,iters}`, recorded through
`arch_metrics.step_active()`, since the optimizer step runs under no_grad.

**Tests:**
- `tests/unit_tests/optimizer/test_stable_rank_floor.py`;
- `test_emerging_optimizers.py`: the `*_stable_rank_floor_*`, `*_spectral_cap_*` and `*_expert_orthogonal` tests;
- `tests/unit_tests/optimizer/test_muon_fsdp_row_sharded.py`: 4 ranks, sharded == unsharded.
