Interpretation

What the checkpoint weights do—and do not—say

Ruled down

Catastrophic corruption, non-finite weights, exact-zero tensors, and dead matrix rows/columns.

Lead weight signature

Mild FP8-specific growth-rate inflection in QKV and attention output projections.

Likely symptom

Norm-tail collapse starts early and accelerates late. Its continuation under BF16 does not exclude damage inherited at the 60k fork.

Still unresolved

The scan is correlational. It does not establish why the loss turns at 66,625.

Bottom line: the weights remain globally coherent. Attention carries the strongest differential signal, but it is a candidate marker—not a causal explanation.
Decision path

Test the attention signature against function

01

Fixed-data loss

Score 60k–72k checkpoints on identical held-out batches and correlate the turn with blockwise drift.

02

Updates + optimizer

Measure update-to-weight ratios and optimizer-state movement. Bucket-only attribution is the current limitation.

03

Attention internals

Join QKV/output drift to activation amax, gradients, and FP8 scales by layer.
